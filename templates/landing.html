✦ Connect Your Data

Bring your business data
into one secure workspace

Complete a guided connection after purchase and start every workspace with the right data ready. DataProvido uses approved sources only to provide the analysis you request.

📊
Google Analytics 4
Read-only access loads the properties and reporting metrics needed for category, product and funnel dashboards. DataProvido cannot edit your Analytics configuration.
🛒
Merchant Center
Google's Merchant content permission is used for read-only account, catalog, availability, performance and pricing reports. DataProvido does not create, update or delete listings.
◎
Own CRM User Footprint
Add consented first-party customer segments and lifecycle signals to understand repeat intent, retention and conversion quality across your own audience.
🔐
Your Control
OAuth sessions are encrypted and expire after 30 days. You can disconnect at any time to revoke Google access and remove the local session.
