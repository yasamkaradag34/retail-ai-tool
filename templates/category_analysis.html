How these metrics are calculated

Items viewed, added to cart and purchased count item quantities reported to GA4. Items purchased are not orders or transactions. Item revenue excludes tax and shipping.

Cart-to-view and purchase-to-view rates are GA4’s user-based rates: users who added or purchased an item divided by users who viewed it. A user can purchase without a recorded view, so these are not an ordered funnel.

Bounce rate is the share of sessions that were not engaged. Engagement rate is its complement. Average session duration is returned by GA4 in seconds. The cards above show the whole property; row metrics describe the associated item breakdown only when supported.

Period changes compare the immediately preceding, equally long date range. Rate changes use percentage points (pp). Zero denominators or missing prior data show a dash. Event counts below describe activity, not individual user journeys.
