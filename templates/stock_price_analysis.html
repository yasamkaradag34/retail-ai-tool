How this workspace uses Merchant Center data

Below market benchmark means your current price is more than 3% below Google’s benchmark price. Above market benchmark means it is more than 3% above. Products within ±3% are labelled competitively priced. The threshold is a DataProvido presentation rule; Google supplies the two prices.

Catalog and benchmark values are snapshots. Date controls apply to Shopping clicks, impressions and performance. Changes compare the immediately preceding period of equal length. Merchant conversion metrics are available only for free listings, so conversion columns are labelled accordingly.

Availability comes from product data in Merchant Center. It can identify in-stock, out-of-stock, preorder and backorder states, but it does not provide warehouse quantity, reorder points or days of cover. Those require an ERP or inventory feed.

Price suggestions and predicted changes appear only when Google returns eligible Price Insights rows. Market Insights reports also depend on account eligibility, role permissions, sufficient market activity and Google’s reporting terms.
