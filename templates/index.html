Why Local AI
protects your data.

🏠 Runs 100% on Your Machine

Every query, every answer, every analysis happens exclusively on your local hardware. No request ever leaves your network. Your business data never touches an external server — not ours, not anyone else's.

📡 No Internet Required

Retail AI operates fully offline after initial setup. This means you can run sensitive stock, sales, and customer analyses in air-gapped environments, on factory floors, or in regions with restricted connectivity — with zero risk of data interception.

Air-gap compatible No cloud dependency Works on-premise LAN-only deployable

🧠 The Model Learns Nothing About You

LLaMA 3.1 runs as a stateless process. Conversation history is stored only in your local session memory and is never used for model training or telemetry. Reset the chat and it's gone — permanently.

🇪🇺 GDPR & Compliance Ready

Because no personal data is transferred outside your infrastructure, compliance with GDPR, KVKK (Turkey), CCPA, and sector-specific regulations becomes dramatically simpler. You remain the sole data controller.

GDPR Article 25 KVKK compliant No third-party processors

🔑 You Own the Keys

Your Excel files, product SKUs, order histories, and customer data remain on your disk. No API key tied to a cloud provider. No subscription that could be revoked. No vendor lock-in on your own business intelligence.
