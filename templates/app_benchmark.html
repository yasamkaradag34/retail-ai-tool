How to read these comparisons

Customer rating and rating footprint use the storefront’s current rating and rating count. Update recency is the number of days since the latest version. Localization reach is the number of languages listed by the store.

A 1.20× ratio means the observed value is 20% higher than your baseline. Rating footprint is not an install count, and language count does not measure usage.

How sentiment and review counts are calculated

Each public written review is classified from its star rating: 4–5 stars positive, 3 stars neutral, and 1–2 stars negative. This keeps the comparison consistent across languages.

The review count shown here is the number of recent written reviews available in Apple’s public feed, up to 50 per app. It is a sample and will usually be lower than the storefront’s total rating count.

Sources & data availabilityApple App Store public APIs

Search and comparisons use Apple’s public Search / Lookup API. Written reviews use Apple’s public customer-review feed for the selected storefront. Availability varies by app and country; missing values are shown as “Not available”. Ratings count ratings, while the review sentiment section counts only written reviews in the retrieved sample.

Exact installs, uninstalls, active users, sessions, retention and revenue are not public store data and are intentionally excluded. Google Play does not provide an official public competitor API; its public listing fields require a separate store-page integration.
